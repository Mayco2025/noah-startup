How EthioMirai LLC handles personal information.

EthioMirai LLC (“we”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our contact or inquiry forms, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your inquiry.

2. How We Use It

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, provide and administer our services (such as courses and consultations), and improve our services. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.

3. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except when required by law or with your consent. Form submissions are processed by our hosting provider (Netlify) solely to deliver them to us.

4. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorized access, and disclosure.

5. Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time using the contact details below. We will respond promptly after confirming your identity.
